Capstone Defense Reviewer: Why These Graphs Fit the System

Integrated POS-Driven Inventory System | Sales, inventory, product movement, promotions, and customer behavior

The graphs were selected as decision-support tools, not decorative charts. Each visualization answers a real store question: what is selling, what is not moving, which sizes need restocking, and whether promotions are profitable.
Sales Trend

Sales Performance Over Time

  • Best for showing revenue and units sold across time.
  • Shows whether sales are increasing, decreasing, or stable.
  • Works with daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual views.
Defense answer: We used a line graph because sales are continuous over time. It helps the owner identify trends and compare revenue against units sold for the selected period.
Inventory

Inventory Turnover Graph

  • Shows how efficiently stock is being sold.
  • Helps detect fast movers, slow movers, and overstock.
  • Supports restocking and markdown decisions.
Defense answer: Inventory turnover is important because the system is inventory-driven. It tells the store if stock is moving efficiently or if products are staying too long in inventory.
Shoe Sizes

Size Curve Bubble Heatmap

  • Rows show product styles; columns show shoe sizes.
  • Bubble size shows stock depth.
  • Color shows movement or sales velocity.
Defense answer: This is best for a shoe store because shoe inventory is size-sensitive. It helps prevent stockouts in popular sizes and identifies sizes that may become dead stock.
Promotions

Promotion Analytics

  • Measures revenue and units sold under campaigns.
  • Compares performance against target sales goals.
  • Helps decide whether to continue, adjust, or end a promo.
Defense answer: Promotion analytics proves whether discounts are effective. The system checks if a promotion generates enough sales without simply lowering prices blindly.

Why These Are the Best Graphs for This Capstone

✓They match the system goal: POS-driven inventory decisions.
✓They support real shoe-store workflows, especially size-based stock control.
✓They connect sales, stock, products, and promotions instead of treating them separately.
✓They help administrators and inventory staff act faster using visual evidence.
Final defense statement: These graphs were chosen because they directly solve the system’s business problems: tracking sales trends, monitoring inventory movement, identifying size-level stock issues, and measuring promotion effectiveness. They turn POS transactions into useful management insights.
